About us

A place to start speaking English with confidence.

NIMURA English Course is a simulated English-learning institution created to help students and young professionals develop practical English skills. We believe language grows through meaningful practice, encouragement, and consistent learning.

Our Vision

To become a welcoming English-learning community that empowers learners to communicate confidently and access wider opportunities.

Our Mission

  • Provide accessible and engaging English lessons.
  • Develop speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills.
  • Encourage confidence through guided practice and feedback.
  • Connect classroom learning with everyday and academic needs.

Course programs

Find the program that fits your goal

Sample programs and fees for this course project. Schedule and fees can be adjusted.

Beginner • A1–A2

English for Beginners

Build a strong foundation for everyday English.

  • Target: Teens and adults, beginner level
  • Goals: Introductions, simple conversations, basic comprehension
  • Topics: Vocabulary, grammar basics, pronunciation
  • Duration: 8 weeks • 2 meetings/week
  • Method: Guided practice, pair work, mini tasks
  • Schedule: Tue & Thu, 16:00–17:30
  • Simulated fee: Rp350.000
  • Facilities: Learning materials and progress notes
  • Evaluation: Weekly practice and final speaking task
  • Outcome: Certificate of completion
Pre-intermediate • A2–B1

English Conversation

Speak more naturally in familiar situations.

  • Target: Students and young professionals
  • Goals: Improve fluency, confidence, and listening
  • Topics: Daily life, opinions, storytelling, role-play
  • Duration: 8 weeks • 2 meetings/week
  • Method: Discussion, role-play, speaking games
  • Schedule: Mon & Wed, 16:00–17:30
  • Simulated fee: Rp400.000
  • Facilities: Speaking worksheets and feedback
  • Evaluation: Recorded speaking tasks and interview
  • Outcome: Certificate of completion
Academic • A2–B2

English for Academic Purposes

Prepare to use English in study and academic settings.

  • Target: University and college students
  • Goals: Strengthen academic reading, writing, and presentation
  • Topics: Note-taking, paragraph writing, presentations
  • Duration: 10 weeks • 2 meetings/week
  • Method: Workshops, peer review, presentations
  • Schedule: Sat & Sun, 09:00–10:30
  • Simulated fee: Rp500.000
  • Facilities: Digital modules and writing feedback
  • Evaluation: Portfolio and final presentation
  • Outcome: Certificate and learning portfolio
